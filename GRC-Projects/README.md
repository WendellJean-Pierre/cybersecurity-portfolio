## Project 1: Cybersecurity Risk Assessment and Risk Register

**Status:** Planned

**Project Type:** Simulated GRC Case Study

**Objective:** Evaluate cybersecurity risks within a fictional organization, assess their likelihood and business impact, and recommend appropriate security controls.

### Planned Project Activities

1. Define the organization's business environment and critical assets.
2. Identify cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities.
3. Assess risk likelihood and potential impact.
4. Develop a cybersecurity risk register.
5. Recommend security controls and risk mitigation measures.
6. Document findings and recommendations.

### Reference Frameworks

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-30, Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments
- ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management

### Expected Deliverables

- Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Report
- Risk Register
- Security Control Recommendations
- Executive Summary
