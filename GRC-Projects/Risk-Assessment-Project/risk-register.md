**Status:** Draft, pending assessment

## Risk Scoring Method

Likelihood: 1 (Rare) to 5 (Almost Certain)
Impact: 1 (Insignificant) to 5 (Severe)
Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

- Low: 1-5
- Medium: 6-10
- High: 11-15
- Critical: 16-25

All scores below are illustrative assumptions, not findings from a real assessment.
