## 3. Remediation Status Definitions

- Open: Finding documented; corrective action not started.
- In Progress: Corrective action underway.
- Pending Verification: Corrective action reported complete; validation outstanding.
- Closed: Corrective action verified and documented.
- Risk Accepted: Authorized decision to retain the risk, with documented justification.
