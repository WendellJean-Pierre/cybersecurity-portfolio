# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment and Risk Register

**Prepared by:** Wendell Jean-Pierre

**Project Type:** Simulated GRC Case Study

**Status:** In Progress

## 1. Project Overview

This project examines cybersecurity risks at a fictional healthcare organization, **Clearview Community Health**, which provides outpatient medical services and handles sensitive patient information.

The assessment focuses on identifying information security risks, evaluating their likelihood and impact, recommending appropriate safeguards, and documenting findings in a risk register.

All scenarios and information used in this project are fictional. No real patient or employer data is included.

## 2. Business Environment

Clearview Community Health is a fictional outpatient healthcare provider with 75 employees, two clinic locations, an electronic health record (EHR) system, a patient scheduling platform, and cloud-based email services.

Critical assets include:
- Electronic health records and protected health information (PHI)
- Patient scheduling and registration systems
- Employee accounts and access permissions
- Workstations, networks, and cloud services
- Data backups and recovery procedures

## 3. Assessment Objectives

1. Identify threats and vulnerabilities affecting critical systems.
2. Evaluate risk likelihood and business impact.
3. Prioritize risks using a consistent scoring method.
4. Recommend practical security controls and mitigation actions.
5. Document risks, owners, and proposed treatment decisions in a risk register.

## 4. Risk Assessment Methodology

This project uses a simplified qualitative risk assessment approach informed by NIST SP 800-30.

Each risk will receive:

- **Likelihood:** Rare (1), Unlikely (2), Possible (3), Likely (4), or Almost Certain (5)
- **Impact:** Insignificant (1), Minor (2), Moderate (3), Major (4), or Severe (5)
- **Risk Score:** Likelihood × Impact

Risk scores are grouped as follows:

- **Low:** 1–4
- **Medium:** 5–9
- **High:** 10–15
- **Critical:** 16–25

These scoring thresholds are defined for this simulated exercise and are not an official NIST scoring scale.

## 5. Initial Risk Scenarios

The assessment will examine:

1. Phishing attacks targeting employee accounts.
2. Unauthorized access to patient information.
3. Ransomware affecting clinical operations.
4. Inadequate backup and recovery procedures.
5. Delayed security updates on employee workstations.

## 6. Relevant Frameworks and Requirements

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- NIST SP 800-30, Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments
- HIPAA Security Rule, as applicable to the fictional healthcare environment
- ISO/IEC 27001, as a reference for information security management practices
