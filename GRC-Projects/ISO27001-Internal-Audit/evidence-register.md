## 1. Purpose

This evidence register tracks the documents and records requested for the simulated ISO 27001 internal security audit.

The register supports evidence collection, traceability, and evaluation across three audit areas:

1. Information security policies and governance
2. Risk assessment and risk treatment
3. Identity and access management

All evidence used in this project will be fictional or synthetic.

## 2. Evidence Status Definitions

- **Requested:** Evidence has been identified for collection but has not been provided.
- **Received:** Synthetic evidence has been made available but has not yet been evaluated.
- **Reviewed:** Evidence has been examined and an observation recorded.
- **Incomplete:** Evidence has been reviewed but additional information is required.
- **Not Available:** Requested evidence could not be provided within the simulated scenario.

Evidence status describes the collection and review process. It does not automatically determine whether a requirement or control is met.

## 6. Evidence Evaluation Principles

Each evidence item will be evaluated for:

- **Relevance:** Does the evidence address the applicable audit question?
- **Reliability:** Is the evidence credible within the fictional scenario?
- **Completeness:** Does the evidence cover the required activity or process?
- **Consistency:** Does the evidence agree with related records?
- **Timeliness:** Is the evidence appropriate for the simulated audit period?

A document's existence does not prove that the underlying control is operating effectively.

## 7. Evidence Handling Rules

- Use fictional organizations, employees, and stakeholders.
- Do not upload real patient information or employee credentials.
- Clearly identify synthetic documents and simulated observations.
- Record evidence limitations.
- Do not invent completed testing or claim real-world verification.
- Maintain traceability between evidence IDs, checklist items, and audit findings.
