# ISO 27001 Internal Security Audit

**Prepared by:** Wendell Jean-Pierre  
**Project Type:** Simulated Cybersecurity GRC Portfolio Project  
**Organization:** Clearview Community Health (Fictional)  
**Industry:** Healthcare  
**Standard:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
**Project Status:** In Progress

## 1. Project Overview

This project simulates an internal information security audit for Clearview Community Health, a fictional healthcare organization with approximately 75 employees and two outpatient clinics.

The purpose is to evaluate selected information security management system (ISMS) requirements and security controls using defined audit criteria, synthetic evidence, and structured assessment procedures.

This project demonstrates practical skills relevant to cybersecurity governance, risk, compliance, and internal auditing.

## 2. Business Environment

Clearview Community Health uses electronic health records (EHR), patient scheduling systems, cloud email, and employee workstations.

The organization handles sensitive patient information, employee records, and authentication credentials.

Fictional stakeholders include the IT Manager, Privacy Officer, Compliance Officer, and Clinic Administrator.

## 3. Audit Objectives

1. Establish an internal audit scope and criteria.
2. Identify relevant ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
3. Develop an audit checklist and evidence requests.
4. Review synthetic evidence against selected requirements.
5. Document supported findings and control gaps.
6. Recommend corrective actions and responsible owners.
7. Prepare a management report.

## 4. Audit Scope

Proposed areas for assessment:

- Information security governance and documented policies
- Information security risk assessment and treatment
- Identity and access management
- Security awareness and training
- Audit evidence and corrective action management

Out of scope:

- Production penetration testing or vulnerability scanning
- Access to actual patient or employee records
- Real employee interviews
- ISO certification decisions
- Formal legal or regulatory compliance opinions

## 5. Audit Framework

**Primary standard:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Selected requirements for consideration:

- Clause 4.3: Scope of the ISMS
- Clause 6.1.2: Information security risk assessment
- Clause 6.1.3: Information security risk treatment
- Clause 7.5: Documented information
- Clause 9.2.1: Internal audit
- Clause 9.2.2: Internal audit programme
- Clause 10.2: Nonconformity and corrective action

Relevant Annex A controls may be included based on the simulated organization's risk treatment decisions and Statement of Applicability.

ISO/IEC 27002:2022 provides supporting implementation guidance.

## 6. Audit Methodology

The simulated audit will follow these stages:

1. Define scope and audit criteria.
2. Prepare an audit plan.
3. Create an evidence request checklist.
4. Review synthetic documents and records.
5. Evaluate evidence against audit criteria.
6. Record supported findings and corrective actions.
7. Prepare a management report.

Missing evidence will be documented as an evidence limitation until the applicable requirement and available evidence have been evaluated.
