# ISO 27001 Internal Security Audit Plan

**Prepared by:** Wendell Jean-Pierre  
**Organization:** Clearview Community Health (Fictional)  
**Audit Type:** Simulated Internal Security Audit  
**Standard:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022  
**Project Status:** Planning  
**Audit Reference:** CCH-IA-001

## 1. Audit Purpose

The purpose of this simulated internal security audit is to evaluate selected information security management system (ISMS) requirements and related controls at Clearview Community Health.

The assessment will focus on security governance, risk management, and identity and access management.

The audit will use fictional organizational records and synthetic evidence to practice evidence-based control evaluation, audit finding documentation, and corrective action planning.

## 2. Organization Background

Clearview Community Health is a fictional healthcare organization operating two outpatient clinics with approximately 75 employees.

Its business environment includes:

- Electronic health records (EHR)
- Patient scheduling systems
- Cloud-based email
- Employee workstations
- Sensitive patient and employee information

The organization requires appropriate security governance and access controls to protect its information and support clinical operations.

## 3. Audit Objectives

1. Evaluate whether selected information security policies are documented, approved, communicated, and maintained.
2. Assess whether information security risks are identified, evaluated, and addressed through a documented risk management process.
3. Examine whether access to sensitive systems is authorized, reviewed, and appropriately restricted.
4. Identify supported control gaps or nonconformities based on the available simulated evidence.
5. Recommend corrective actions, responsible owners, and proposed completion targets.
6. Prepare an audit report suitable for simulated management review.

## 4. Audit Scope

The audit will examine three selected areas.

### Area 1: Information Security Policies and Governance

Evaluate the availability, approval, communication, and review of information security policies.

### Area 2: Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment

Evaluate documented risk assessment methods, risk evaluation records, risk treatment decisions, and assigned responsibilities.

### Area 3: Identity and Access Management

Evaluate user account authorization, access restrictions, authentication practices, and periodic access reviews.

### Out of Scope

- Live penetration testing
- Production vulnerability scanning
- Access to real patient information
- Actual employee interviews
- Formal ISO certification decisions
- Comprehensive testing of every ISO 27001 requirement

## 5. Audit Criteria

Selected ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements and Annex A controls will guide the audit.

| Audit Area | Reference | Assessment Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Governance | Annex A 5.1 | Policies for information security |
| Risk management | Clause 6.1.2 | Information security risk assessment |
| Risk treatment | Clause 6.1.3 | Information security risk treatment |
| Access control | Annex A 5.15 | Access control |
| Identity management | Annex A 5.16 | Identity management |
| Access rights | Annex A 5.18 | Access rights |
| Authentication | Annex A 8.5 | Secure authentication |

Relevant Annex A controls will be evaluated against their applicability and the fictional organization's documented requirements. ISO/IEC 27002:2022 may provide supporting implementation guidance.

## 6. Audit Approach

The simulated audit will use the following procedures:

1. **Document review:** Examine fictional policies, procedures, risk records, and access management documentation.
2. **Evidence inspection:** Evaluate synthetic approval records, access review records, and risk treatment documentation.
3. **Criteria comparison:** Compare the evidence with applicable audit criteria.
4. **Gap identification:** Record potential deficiencies and distinguish missing evidence from demonstrated nonconformities.
5. **Finding documentation:** Describe supported findings, associated risks, and recommended corrective actions.
6. **Management reporting:** Summarize results, limitations, and proposed remediation priorities.

No live system testing or real stakeholder interviews will be performed.

## 10. Audit Limitations

This project uses synthetic evidence and fictional organizational information.

No actual organizational systems will be accessed, and no real employees will be interviewed.

The exercise does not establish ISO 27001 conformity for a real organization or represent an independent certification audit.

Because the portfolio author also develops the simulated materials, the exercise does not establish the auditor independence required of an actual internal audit programme.
